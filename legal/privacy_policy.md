## 1. Introduction

EthicBids Technologies™ ("we", "our", "us") operates the Cyber Defense Platform ("Platform"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard personal information when you use our Platform.

By using the Platform, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide
- Account registration details (username, email address, password hash)
- Profile information (organization, team membership)
- Challenge submissions and flag attempts
- Support requests and communications

### B. Information Collected Automatically
- IP addresses and device identifiers
- Browser type and operating system
- Platform activity logs (page views, challenge interactions, login events)
- Session tokens (stored as encrypted cookies)

### C. Information We Do Not Collect
- Payment card numbers (processed by third-party payment providers)
- Government ID numbers
- Biometric data

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- To provide, operate, and improve the Platform
- To authenticate your identity and maintain session security
- To generate leaderboard rankings and progress tracking
- To send platform announcements and security alerts
- To comply with legal obligations
- To detect and prevent fraudulent or malicious activity

---

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share data with:
- **Service Providers**: Cloud hosting, database providers (under DPA agreements)
- **Legal Authorities**: When required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety
