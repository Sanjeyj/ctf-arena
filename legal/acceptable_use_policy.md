# Acceptable Use Policy
# EthicBids Technologies™ | Cyber Defense Platform
# Effective Date: 2026-07-17

---

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) defines the standards of conduct expected of all Platform users. Violations may result in account suspension or termination.

---

## 2. Competition Integrity

- **No flag sharing**: Do not share flags, partial solutions, or hints with other participants during active competitions.
- **No automated solvers**: Automated flag-brute-force scripts against production challenge infrastructure are prohibited.
- **No exploitation of platform bugs**: Vulnerabilities discovered in the Platform must be reported via our responsible disclosure process — not exploited for competitive advantage.
- **Fair Play**: All participants must rely on their own knowledge and skills.

---

## 3. Content Standards

Users must not post, upload, or transmit:
- Malware, ransomware, or destructive payloads
- Content that is defamatory, harassing, or discriminatory
- Personally identifiable information of third parties without consent
- Content that violates intellectual property rights

---

## 4. Security Research Standards

For self-hosted instances, users may conduct security research within their authorized scope. Any testing must:
- Be limited to systems you own or have explicit written authorization to test
- Not impact other users' data or availability
- Follow responsible disclosure if vulnerabilities are discovered

---

## 5. Reporting Violations

Report AUP violations to: **trust@ethicbids.app**  
Report security vulnerabilities to: **security@ethicbids.app** (see Responsible Disclosure Policy)

---

## 6. Enforcement

Violations are subject to:
1. Warning notice
2. Temporary suspension (24–72 hours)
3. Permanent account ban
4. Legal action (for severe violations)
