# Responsible AI Policy | EthicBids Technologies™
**Effective:** 2026-07-17

## Principles

1. **Human Oversight**: All AI-generated recommendations (v2.0 AI Copilot) require human review before action. AI systems do not autonomously modify production systems.
2. **Transparency**: Users are notified when they interact with AI-generated content or AI-assisted scoring.
3. **Fairness**: AI models are evaluated for bias across participant demographics before deployment.
4. **Data Minimization**: AI models are trained only on data with explicit authorization. Personal data is anonymized before model training.
5. **Explainability**: AI recommendations include confidence scores and reasoning traces where technically feasible.
6. **No Autonomous Harm**: AI agents (v2.0 research) are sandboxed and cannot autonomously launch attacks, modify databases, or change authentication.

## Prohibited AI Uses
- Generating misleading security certifications
- Autonomous flag validation circumvention
- Personal data profiling without consent
- AI-generated security audit reports presented as human-verified without disclosure

## Governance
AI systems are reviewed by the Architecture Review Board before production deployment.
