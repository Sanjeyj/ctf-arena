# Data Retention Policy | EthicBids Technologies™
**Effective:** 2026-07-17

| Category | Retention | Disposal |
|---|---|---|
| User account data | Account lifetime + 90 days | Secure deletion (AES overwrite) |
| Activity logs | 90 days rolling | Automated purge |
| Challenge submissions | Duration of competition + 1 year | Anonymized archive |
| Backup archives | 30 days (local), 90 days (Glacier) | S3 lifecycle expiry |
| Support tickets | 2 years | Secure deletion |
| Financial records | 7 years | Secure archive |
| Security audit logs | 1 year | Secure deletion |

**Data Deletion Requests**: privacy@ethicbids.app (processed within 30 days).
