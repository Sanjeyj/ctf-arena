# Security Investment & ROI Analytics — CDP v2.0

## 1. Return on Security Investment (ROSI)

The platform evaluates the ROI of security investments by comparing mitigation costs with risk reductions:

```
ROSI = (Risk Exposure Reduction - Mitigation Cost) / Mitigation Cost
```

---

## 2. Decision Optimization

- **Portfolio Frontier**: Identifies control investment portfolios that maximize risk reduction for a given budget.
- **Risk Mitigation Score**: Evaluates control effectiveness against MITRE ATT&CK techniques on the knowledge graph.
- **ROI Analytics**: Visualizes ROSI indices to assist with strategic planning.
