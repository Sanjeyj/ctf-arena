# Break-Even Analysis | EthicBids Technologies™

## Monthly Fixed Costs (Year 1)

| Cost | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Engineering salaries | $16,000 |
| Operations & support | $6,000 |
| Sales & marketing | $5,000 |
| Infrastructure | $2,500 |
| Legal & compliance | $1,500 |
| **Total Fixed Monthly** | **$31,000** |

## Variable Costs (per customer/month)

| Tier | Variable Cost | Gross Margin |
|---|---|---|
| Starter ($99) | $20 | 80% |
| Professional ($499) | $100 | 80% |
| Enterprise ($2,500 avg) | $375 | 85% |

## Break-Even Point

At a blended average MRR of $350/customer:
- **Break-even customers**: 31,000 ÷ 350 × (1 ÷ 0.80) = **~111 customers**
- **Break-even timeline**: Month 8 (projecting 15 new customers/month with Series A support)
