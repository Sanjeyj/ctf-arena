# Competition Participant Guide
# CTF Arena v1.0.0 — EthicBids Technologies™

Welcome to the CTF Arena! This guide helps you navigate the competition platform, view challenges, submit flags, and track your ranking.

---

## 1. Account Setup

### Registration
1. Navigate to the landing page at `https://your-domain.com/`.
2. Click **Register** on the top navigation bar.
3. Fill in a unique username, your email address, and a secure password.
4. After submission, you will be automatically logged in and redirected to the main Dashboard.

### Login
- Registered users can log in at `https://your-domain.com/login` using their username and password.

---

## 2. Solving Challenges

### Dashboard & Categories
The dashboard groups all active wargaming challenges by category:
- **Web**: Web application security vulnerabilities.
- **Reverse**: Binary reverse engineering.
- **Crypto**: Cryptographic analysis and decryptions.
- **Forensics/Stego**: Steganography and disk/network file analysis.
- **Pwn**: Memory corruption exploitation.

### View Challenge Details
1. Click on a challenge card to open its detail window.
2. Read the prompt, instructions, and any attached resource links.
3. If hints are available, you can click **Unlock Hint** (some hints may deduct points from your score).

---

## 3. Submitting Flags & Scoring

### Flag Format
All flags follow the standard platform format unless stated otherwise in the description:
```
flag{your_flag_text_here}
```
*Note: Flags are case-sensitive and must be entered exactly as found.*

### Submitting a Solution
1. Paste your flag into the text field inside the challenge details window.
2. Click **Submit Flag**.
3. If correct, the banner will turn green, points will be added to your score, and the challenge will be marked as Solved.

### Dynamic Scoring
* Many challenges use dynamic scoring. The point value decreases as more participants solve the challenge. Your total score may change throughout the competition as a result.

---

## 4. Scoreboard & Statistics

- Navigate to the **Scoreboard** page via the main navigation bar.
- The scoreboard displays real-time team rankings, total points, and solve times.
- Solves are resolved in chronological order (the player who reached the score first holds the higher rank).
