## Key Market Trends

1. **Regulatory pressure**: GDPR, DORA (EU), SEBI/RBI guidelines (India) forcing compliance evidence collection.
2. **SOC talent shortage**: 3.5M unfilled cybersecurity jobs globally — organizations must upskill internally.
3. **Gamification of learning**: Engagement rates for CTF-style training 3x higher than video courses.
4. **Consolidation demand**: CISOs prefer integrated platforms over 5+ point solutions.
5. **AI integration**: Enterprises willing to pay 40% premium for AI-assisted security tooling.
