# Community Guidelines | EthicBids Technologies™

## Our Values

1. **Respect**: Treat all community members with dignity. Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks will not be tolerated.
2. **Integrity**: Do not share flags, exploit platform bugs for advantage, or misrepresent your work.
3. **Collaboration**: Help others learn. Share knowledge through writeups (after competitions close). Celebrate others' achievements.
4. **Ethics**: EthicBids is named for ethical practice. All security research and discussion must be conducted legally and responsibly.

## Conduct Standards

✅ **Do**: Share learning resources, ask questions, provide constructive feedback, report bugs responsibly.

❌ **Don't**: Share active challenge flags, engage in hate speech, spam, impersonate others, or conduct unauthorized testing.

## Enforcement

| Violation | Consequence |
|---|---|
| First minor offense | Warning |
| Repeated minor offense | 7-day ban |
| Serious offense | Permanent ban |
| Legal violation | Referral to authorities |

## Reporting

Report violations to: **community@ethicbids.app**  
All reports are reviewed within 24 hours and treated confidentially.
