# Bug Bounty Program | EthicBids Technologies™

## Scope

### In-Scope Systems
- `ethicbids.vercel.app` (Production SaaS)
- `api.ethicbids.app` (API endpoints)
- `ctf-arena` GitHub repository (open-core code)

### Out-of-Scope
- Third-party infrastructure (Vercel, AWS)
- Social engineering attacks on employees
- Physical security
- Rate-limiting or DoS attacks

## Reward Tiers

| Severity | CVSS Score | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| **Critical** | 9.0 – 10.0 | $1,000 – $5,000 |
| **High** | 7.0 – 8.9 | $250 – $1,000 |
| **Medium** | 4.0 – 6.9 | $50 – $250 |
| **Low** | 0.1 – 3.9 | Recognition + swag |
| **Informational** | 0.0 | Hall of Fame credit |

## Submission Process

1. Email: **security@ethicbids.app** with subject: `[BUG BOUNTY] [Severity] Brief Description`
2. Include: Reproduction steps, impact assessment, CVSS score
3. EthicBids acknowledges within **24 hours**
4. Triage within **72 hours**
5. Patch within **7 days** (Critical) / **30 days** (High/Medium)
6. Reward paid within **14 days** of fix verification

## Hall of Fame
Outstanding researchers are listed at `ethicbids.app/security/hof`.

**Responsible Disclosure**: Do not publicly disclose vulnerabilities before we've had 90 days to remediate.
