# Responsible Disclosure Policy
# CTF Arena v1.0.0 — EthicBids Technologies™

EthicBids Technologies™ welcomes security researchers to identify vulnerabilities and help secure the CTF Arena platform. This policy defines the guidelines for reporting security bugs.

---

## 1. Submission Guidelines

- Submit findings to **security@ethicbids.vercel.app**.
- Include a detailed description of the vulnerability, step-by-step reproduction steps, and potential impact.
- Do not exploit the vulnerability further than necessary to prove its existence (e.g. do not access other user data).

---

## 2. Scope & Safe Harbor

### In Scope
* Vulnerabilities in the core web application (RCE, SQLi, XSS, Broken Access Control).
* Configuration issues leading to exposure of sensitive files.

### Out of Scope
* DDoS or resources exhaustion attacks.
* Spam or social engineering.

**Safe Harbor**: EthicBids Technologies™ will not pursue legal action against researchers who adhere to this policy and disclose findings responsibly.

---

## 3. Review Timeline & Disclosure

- **Acknowledgment**: Within 48 hours.
- **Triage & Validation**: Within 5 business days.
- **Remediation**: Standard target of 30 days for public patch.
- **Public Disclosure**: Coordinated public disclosure once patches are available.
