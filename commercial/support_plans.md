# Support & Maintenance Plans
# CTF Arena v1.0.0 — EthicBids Technologies™

This document outlines the support tiers, communication channels, and SLA matrices for commercial support plans.

---

## 1. Support Tiers

### A. Bronze Support (Included with Academic / Event License)
* **Hours**: 09:00 – 17:00 local time, Mon-Fri.
* **Channels**: Email support ticket gateway.
* **SLA**: Next Business Day (NBD).

### B. Silver Support (Included with Professional License)
* **Hours**: 08:00 – 18:00 local time, Mon-Fri.
* **Channels**: Email and dedicated Slack channel.
* **SLA**: **4 Hours** for P1 incidents.

### C. Gold Support (Enterprise Exclusive)
* **Hours**: 24x7x365.
* **Channels**: Hot-line phone support, dedicated Slack, and email.
* **SLA**: **1 Hour** for P1 incidents.
