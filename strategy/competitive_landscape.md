# Competitive Landscape — Long-Term View
# EthicBids Technologies™ | Strategy

## Market Structure (2026)

The cyber training market is fragmented across three tiers:

**Tier 1 — Enterprise Platforms** (> $1B ARR): Immersive Labs, RangeForce — deeply embedded in Fortune 500 but expensive, rigid, and slow to innovate.

**Tier 2 — Community Platforms** ($50M–$500M ARR): HackTheBox, TryHackMe — strong with individual learners but lack enterprise GRC and white-label.

**Tier 3 — Open Source / Niche** (< $50M ARR): CTFd, PicoCTF, OWASP WebGoat — free but no commercial support, no GRC, no SLA.

**EthicBids position**: Attacking the undefended middle — better than Tier 3 (enterprise-grade), cheaper than Tier 1, more comprehensive than Tier 2.
