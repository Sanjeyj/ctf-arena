# Security Digital Twin Simulations Guide

This guide details what-if security twin simulation parameters.

---

## 1. Simulation Scenarios
Supported scenarios include:
- `ransomware`: Models propagation over organizational assets.
- `asset_failure`: Simulates business downtime impact scores.
- `control_failures`: Models risk escalations when compliance controls fail.
