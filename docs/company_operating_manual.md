# EthicBids Technologies™ Company Operating Manual
**Version:** 1.0.0
**Branding:** EthicBids Technologies™
**Focus:** Enterprise Business Operations

---

## 1. Company Operations & Internal Governance

### 1.1 Organizational Responsibilities
- **Executive (CEO/CFO)**: Responsible for overall business strategy, investor relations, resource allocation, and annual budget planning.
- **Product Management (VP of Product)**: Manages roadmap prioritization, pricing changes, and ADR approvals.
- **Sales & Customer Success (VP of Sales)**: Leads customer onboarding lifecycles, SLA renewals, and CRM pipelines.
- **Security & Engineering (VP of Engineering)**: Handles research sandbox supervision, infrastructure updates, and EOL lifecycles.

### 1.2 Meeting Cadence
- **Weekly Syncs**: Mondays 09:00 EST. Reviewing sales pipeline stages and support tickets.
- **Monthly Business Review (MBR)**: First Tuesday of the month. Reviewing MRR, CAC, and LTV metrics.
- **Quarterly Business Review (QBR)**: Performance scores, SLA breaches, and compliance updates.

## 2. Customer & Sales Operations Playbooks

### 2.1 Lead Qualification & CRM Stages
```
[Inbound / Outbound Lead] ──> [BANT Qualification Check] ──> [Technical Demo]
                                                                     │
                                                                     ▼
[Active SLA Monitoring] <── [Onboarding & Setup] <── [Contract & MSA Sign-Off]
```

### 2.2 Customer Health Scoring Matrix
Health scores are computed monthly (from 1 to 100):
- **Adoption Score (40%)**: Ratio of active users to total purchased licenses.
- **SLA Breach Score (30%)**: Number of severity-2/severity-3 SLA escalations.
- **Feedback Score (30%)**: NPS feedback metrics.

---

## 3. Financial & Budget Planning

### 3.1 Revenue Model & Metrics
- **Main Driver**: Annual Subscription Licenses for Cyber Range instances (CDP Enterprise LTS).
- **Core SaaS KPIs**:
  - **MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)**
  - **Churn Rate** (Target < 2% annually)
  - **LTV/CAC Ratio** (Target > 3.5x)

---

## 4. People Operations & employee handbook

### 4.1 Hiring Process
- **Screening**: Technical or operational alignment questionnaire.
- **Panel Interview**: Focuses on structured scoring matrices for team alignment.
- **Decision**: Senior leadership approval required for all hiring offers.

### 4.2 Professional Performance Reviews
Conducted bi-annually focusing on core deliverables, documentation quality, and SLA response execution scorecards.
