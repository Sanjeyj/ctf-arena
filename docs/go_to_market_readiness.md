# Go-To-Market (GTM) & Product Launch Readiness Manual
**Version:** 1.0.0
**Branding:** EthicBids Technologies™
**Product:** Cyber Defense Platform (v1.0.0)

---

## 1. Product Positioning & Elevator Pitch

### 1.1 Unique Value Proposition
The Cyber Defense Platform delivers a secure, multi-role, high-density wargaming cyber range that validates system resilience, threat detection accuracy, and GRC controls. Powered by the AXIOM user experience, CDP translates raw cybersecurity events into measurable risk metrics.

### 1.2 Target Industries & Personas
- **Industries**: Financial Services, Healthcare providers, Defense Industrial Base, Managed Security Service Providers (MSSPs).
- **Personas**:
  - **CISO / CSO (Decision Maker)**: Seeks quantifiable security investments return and team readiness indicators.
  - **SOC Director / Analyst (Operator)**: Focuses on incident response triage, logs analysis, and timeline correlation.
  - **Compliance Officer (Governance)**: Requires continuous control validations against frameworks (NIST, ISO).

## 2. Sales Enablement One-Pager

### 2.1 The 15-Second Pitch
"CDP is an enterprise-grade Cyber Range OS that runs wargames and validations to measure your security posture in real-time, providing immediate visibility into detection gaps and risk vectors."

### 2.2 Pricing Framework Matrix
- **Academic Edition**: Low-density host bounds, community support, educational licenses.
- **Team / Professional Edition**: Up to 50 concurrent wargame nodes, email support.
- **Enterprise LTS Edition**: Unlimited scaling, federated scoreboard ingestion, 24/7 SLA support, multi-region replication.

## 3. Training & Certification Syllabus

### 3.1 Course Outlines
- **CDP-ADM-101 (Administrator)**: Installation, configurations, environment overrides, plugin management, and backup configurations.
- **CDP-SOC-201 (SOC Analyst)**: Tracing incident timelines, alert triage, malware investigation workflows, and MITRE graph navigation.
- **CDP-INS-301 (Instructor/Judge)**: Challenge creation, category alignment, scoring policies, and live wargame orchestration.
