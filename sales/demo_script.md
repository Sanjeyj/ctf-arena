# Demo Script — Cyber Defense Platform
# EthicBids Technologies™ | Sales Team Use

---

## Demo Structure (45 minutes)

**0:00 – 5:00** | Introduction & Discovery Recap
- Greet by name, recap pain points from discovery call
- Set agenda: "I'll show you three things today — the challenge engine, the GRC dashboard, and the admin controls."

**5:00 – 15:00** | Participant Experience
- Navigate to `/login` → register as a participant
- Show the Challenges board: "These are mapped to MITRE ATT&CK and OWASP."
- Solve a demo challenge live: "Let's walk through the Web SQLi challenge."
- Show scoreboard updating in real-time

**15:00 – 25:00** | Admin & GRC Dashboard
- Switch to Admin view
- Show challenge management, user management, RBAC controls
- Open GRC dashboard: "Here's where your compliance team lives."
- Show risk register, compliance framework mapping
- "Every challenge your team completes generates audit evidence here."

**25:00 – 35:00** | SOC Simulation & Reporting
- Walk through a SOC scenario exercise
- Show the MITRE ATT&CK mapping sidebar
- Show participant progress reports: "This is what you send to your board."

**35:00 – 40:00** | Deployment & Integration
- Show Docker compose launch: "You can self-host this in 5 minutes."
- Mention SSO/SAML for Enterprise, Vercel cloud option for SaaS

**40:00 – 45:00** | Q&A + Next Steps
- "What questions do you have?"
- Offer 14-day trial with guided onboarding
- "Let's get your team set up this week."

---

## Demo Environment

- URL: Local `http://localhost:5000` or staging instance
- Admin: `admin` / `[demo password]`
- Pre-seeded: 5 challenges, 3 demo participants, sample GRC data
