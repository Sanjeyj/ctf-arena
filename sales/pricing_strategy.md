# Pricing Strategy — Cyber Defense Platform
# EthicBids Technologies™

---

## Pricing Philosophy

**Value-based pricing**: Price reflects the business outcome delivered (compliance readiness, SOC skill improvement, audit evidence) — not feature count. Anchored above pure CTF tools, below enterprise GRC suites.

---

| Tier | Monthly | Annual | Savings |

---

## What Each Tier Includes

### Community (Free, Self-Hosted)
- Up to 50 participants, 25 challenges, core CTF engine
- Community forum support, MIT license

### Starter ($99/mo)
- Up to 100 participants, 50 challenges
- Basic admin dashboard, email support

### Professional ($499/mo)
- Up to 1,000 participants, unlimited challenges
- GRC Suite, SOC module, MITRE ATT&CK mapping
- Prometheus + Grafana observability
- Priority email support, 99.5% SLA

### Enterprise (Custom)
- Unlimited participants and challenges
- White-label branding, SSO/SAML
- Kubernetes Cyber Range (v2.0)
- Dedicated CSM, 99.9% SLA, 24/7 phone support

## Competitive Positioning

We are priced **above** CTFd OSS (free but no support) and **below** RangeForce/Immersive Labs ($2,000–$10,000/month) — capturing the underserved mid-market.
