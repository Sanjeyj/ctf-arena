# Customer Personas — Cyber Defense Platform
# EthicBids Technologies™

## Persona 1 — The Overwhelmed CISO
**Name**: Priya S., CISO  
**Company**: 500-person FinTech startup  
**Pain**: Board wants proof of security training ROI. Compliance audit next quarter. Team of 8 analysts with no structured training program.  
**Goal**: Measurable skill improvement + compliance evidence in one platform.  
**Decision Factor**: Board-level reporting, compliance framework alignment, audit evidence export.

## Persona 2 — The Security Team Lead
**Name**: Marcus T., Security Team Lead  
**Company**: 1,200-person healthcare organization  
**Pain**: Analysts are bored of video courses. Struggling to retain talent. No way to identify skill gaps objectively.  
**Goal**: Engaging, hands-on challenges that keep the team sharp.  
**Decision Factor**: Challenge quality, leaderboard competition, team analytics dashboard.

## Persona 3 — The CTF Organizer
**Name**: Dr. Arjun K., Computer Science Professor  
**Company**: Top-10 Engineering University  
**Pain**: Running annual CTF events on cobbled-together OSS tools. Setup takes weeks. No persistent learning platform.  
**Goal**: Easy-to-deploy, reliable platform for 200-student annual CTF.  
**Decision Factor**: Ease of deployment, cost (education pricing), challenge customization.

## Persona 4 — The GRC Compliance Manager
**Name**: Sarah L., GRC Manager  
**Company**: 3,000-person insurance company  
**Pain**: Security training data lives in one system, compliance evidence in another. Connecting the two is manual and error-prone.  
**Goal**: Single platform where training activity automatically generates compliance evidence.  
**Decision Factor**: NIST/ISO 27001 mapping, automated evidence collection, audit export.

## Persona 5 — The MSSP Owner
**Name**: Alex R., MSSP Founder  
**Company**: Managed Security Services Provider (50 customers)  
**Pain**: Needs a white-label cyber training platform to offer as a value-added service to SMB customers.  
**Goal**: Resellable platform with custom branding per customer.
