# Sales Playbook — Cyber Defense Platform
# EthicBids Technologies™ | Internal Use Only

---

## 1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

| Attribute | Target |
|---|---|
| **Company Size** | 50–5,000 employees |
| **Industry** | Financial Services, Healthcare, Technology, Government, Defense |
| **Buyer** | CISO, VP Security, Security Team Lead |
| **Pain Points** | Compliance gaps, SOC analyst skill shortages, unproven training ROI |
| **Budget** | $5,000–$100,000/year security training budget |

---

## 2. Sales Stages

| Stage | Description | Exit Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Prospecting** | Identify ICP accounts via LinkedIn, security conferences | Contact identified |
| **Discovery** | 30-min call to understand pain, team size, current tools | Pain confirmed, budget scoped |
| **Demo** | 45-min platform demo tailored to their use case | Positive reception, champion identified |
| **Trial** | 14-day free trial with guided onboarding | Trial activated |
| **Proposal** | Custom pricing proposal | Proposal sent |
| **Negotiation** | Legal/procurement review | MSA drafted |
| **Closed-Won** | Contract signed | Invoice issued |

---

## 3. Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We already use HackTheBox/TryHackMe" | "Those are great for individual skill-building. We add GRC alignment and enterprise SOC simulation that those platforms don't offer — your compliance team will love the reporting." |
| "We have limited budget" | "Our Starter plan begins at $99/month. We also offer annual discounts and proof-of-value trials." |
| "We need on-premises" | "Absolutely supported. Our Enterprise self-hosted option runs on your own Kubernetes cluster with full data sovereignty." |
| "How do we know it works?" | "1,609 automated tests all passing. We'll give you a 14-day trial with your own data." |

---

## 4. Discovery Questions

1. How does your team currently validate cybersecurity skills?
2. What compliance frameworks are you required to demonstrate controls for?
3. How many analysts/engineers need regular security training?
4. How do you currently measure training effectiveness for board reporting?
5. What's your biggest security skills gap right now?

---

## 5. Competitive Battle Cards

**vs. CTFd OSS**: "CTFd requires self-hosting, has no GRC, and no commercial support. We're production-ready with enterprise SLAs."

**vs. HackTheBox**: "HackTheBox is excellent for individual growth, but doesn't map to your compliance frameworks or provide white-label enterprise deployments."
