# Long-Term Support (LTS) Policy
# CTF Arena v1.0.0 — EthicBids Technologies™

This document defines the lifecycle support phases and SLAs for the certified v1.0.0 release.

---

## 1. LTS Support Lifecycle

The v1.0.0 release is designated as an LTS release with a **3-year lifecycle**:

```
[ Active Support ] ──► [ Maintenance Support ] ──► [ End of Life (EOL) ]
    12 Months                 24 Months                Post 36 Months
```

### Phase A: Active Support (Months 1 – 12)
* **Status**: General Availability
* **Activities**: Regular feature backports, bug fixes, dependency upgrades, and security patching.
* **Release Cadence**: Bi-weekly.

### Phase B: Maintenance Support (Months 13 – 36)
* **Status**: Critical Maintenance Only
* **Activities**: High/Critical security hotfixes and blocker bug remediation only. No functional additions.
* **Release Cadence**: Monthly / Ad-hoc.

### Phase C: End of Life (Post Month 36)
* **Status**: Unsupported
* **Activities**: No patches, support tickets, or verification tests run. Upgrades to v2.x mandatory.

---

## 2. LTS Commitments

EthicBids Technologies™ guarantees the following SLAs for v1.0.0 LTS customers:

| Service | Active Support SLA | Maintenance Support SLA |
|---------|---------------------|--------------------------|
| **Response Time** | 4 Hours (P1) | 8 Hours (P1) |
| **Hotfix Delivery** | 24 Hours (Critical) | 48 Hours (Critical) |
| **Compatibility** | Certified OS matrix | Certified OS matrix |
