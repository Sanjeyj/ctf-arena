## 1. Quick Start for Competitors

### Sign Up & Login
1. Navigate to the homepage at `https://your-domain.com/`.
2. Click **Register** on the navbar to create your profile.
3. Access active wargaming challenges via the **Challenges** page on the dashboard.

### Flag Verification
- Enter flag strings in the input box inside challenge detail cards.
- The standard format is: `flag{your_flag_text}`.
- If incorrect, the dashboard displays a red warning banner. If correct, the card displays a checkmark and is locked.

### Dynamic Scoring
- Point values decay as more participants solve a challenge. Submit solutions quickly to maximize your points!
