---

## 4. Logo Usage Rules

- Always use the full brand name "EthicBids Technologies™" on first mention in documents.
- "EthicBids" acceptable for subsequent references and UI space-constrained contexts.
- The ™ symbol must always follow "EthicBids Technologies" in formal documentation.
- Do not modify the brand colors, stretch, or recolor the logo.
- Minimum clear space: equal to the cap-height of the "E" in EthicBids on all sides.

## 5. Voice & Tone

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Marketing** | Confident, inspiring | "Dominate the cyber battlefield." |
| **Documentation** | Clear, technical, precise | "Configure `SESSION_COOKIE_SECURE=True` for HTTPS deployments." |
| **Support** | Empathetic, helpful | "Let's get this resolved for you." |
| **Legal** | Formal, unambiguous | "You must not..." |

---

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

✅ **Do**: Use dark backgrounds with cyan accents consistently.  
✅ **Do**: Pair professional imagery with concise, action-oriented copy.  
❌ **Don't**: Use generic stock photography of "hacker in hoodie" clichés.  
❌ **Don't**: Abbreviate "EthicBids" as "EB" in public-facing materials.
