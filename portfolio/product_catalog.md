## Product Lines

### 🛡️ CTF Arena — Core Platform
The foundational Capture-The-Flag competition engine for hands-on cybersecurity training.

**Included:**
- Challenge engine (Web, Crypto, Forensics, RE, Pwn, Steganography, OSINT)
- Real-time scoreboard
- Team & individual competition modes
- Admin dashboard & RBAC
- Participant registration & profile management
- Announcement engine

### 📊 GRC Intelligence Suite
Governance, Risk & Compliance management integrated directly into the training platform.

**Included:**
- Risk register management
- Compliance framework mapping (NIST CSF, ISO 27001, SOC 2, OWASP)
- Audit trail and evidence collection
- Vendor risk assessment
- Security workflow automation

### 🔐 Security Operations Module
Training environment for SOC analyst workflows and incident response.

**Included:**
- Incident response playbooks
- Alert triage training
- Threat hunting exercises
- MITRE ATT&CK mapping
- Observability dashboards (Prometheus + Grafana)
