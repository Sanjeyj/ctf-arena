# Channel Strategy | EthicBids Technologies™

## Go-To-Market Channels

| Channel | 2027 Target | Revenue Mix |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct (Inbound)** | 50% | SEO, Product Hunt, community |
| **Direct (Outbound)** | 25% | Enterprise CISO outreach |
| **Resellers** | 15% | MSSP, VAR channel |
| **Education Partners** | 10% | University, bootcamp |

## Channel Enablement
- Reseller portal (deal registration, marketing materials)
- Partner certification program (technical + sales)
- Monthly partner newsletter and product updates
- Co-sell support for deals > $10,000

## Regional Priority
1. **India** — Home market, early adopter community
2. **Southeast Asia** — High cybersecurity investment growth
3. **Middle East & Africa** — Government and banking sector demand
4. **North America** — Enterprise SaaS premium pricing opportunity
