# Education Partnership Program
# EthicBids Technologies™ | Partners

---

## Program Overview

The EthicBids Academic Alliance empowers universities, cybersecurity bootcamps, and training institutes to deliver world-class cyber defense education using the Cyber Defense Platform.

---

## Benefits for Academic Partners

| Benefit | Details |
|---|---|
| **Free Institutional License** | Up to 200 students per semester, fully-featured |
| **Curriculum Materials** | 12-week course syllabus, lab guides, assessment rubrics |
| **Dedicated Support** | Quarterly review calls with EthicBids education team |
| **Co-Branding** | "Powered by EthicBids" badge and joint marketing opportunities |
| **Student Pathways** | Top students offered discounted Professional subscriptions post-graduation |

---

## Requirements

- Accredited educational institution or recognized training provider
- Minimum 30 students per cohort
- Designated academic liaison contact
- Agreement to provide end-of-semester usage feedback
